Help

Before your first wash.

Still stuck? Our stylists answer every message within a day at hello@fofo.hair

How fast will I see a difference?

Slip and softness are immediate — you’ll feel it on the first wash. Strength and breakage results build over four weekly Bond Mask uses, and scalp-density results over 12 weeks of daily Root Ritual.

Are your products safe for colour-treated and keratin-treated hair?

Yes. Everything is sulfate-free and pH-balanced between 4.5 and 5.5, which helps keep the cuticle closed and colour locked in. Wait 72 hours after a keratin treatment before the first wash.

Do you use silicones?

Only one — a water-soluble amodimethicone in Heat Veil, where it’s the most effective thermal barrier we tested. It rinses clean with Cloud Wash and never builds up.

What if it doesn’t work for my hair?

Use it for up to 90 days. If you don’t love it, send us a note — even if the bottle is empty — and we’ll refund you in full. No return shipping needed.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within one business day. Standard delivery takes 2–5 days and is free over $50; express is $9 and arrives in 1–2 days.
