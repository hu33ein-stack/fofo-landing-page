Brazilian Portuguese for soft, plush, cuddly — the word you'd use for a pillow, a puppy, a good cake. We named the cream after the feeling we were chasing: the exact softness of skin after it's been properly, thoroughly moisturized. How it's used

A two-minute ritual, not a ten-step routine.

fofo is built to be the whole moisturizing step — nothing to layer underneath, nothing to wait on after.

STEP 01

Scoop a pea-sized swirl

The whip expands as it warms — a little goes further than it looks. Scoop straight from the jar with clean fingers or the included spatula.

STEP 02

Press, don't rub

Warm it between your palms for five seconds, then press into cheeks, forehead, and jaw. Pressing helps the whip melt into skin instead of sitting on top.

STEP 03

Let it finish working

No need to wait before makeup or SPF — fofo sinks in fully within sixty seconds and leaves a soft, non-greasy finish underneath.

Questions

Before you scoop.

Is fofo actually a whipped texture, or is that just marketing?+

It's air-whipped during manufacturing, the same way you'd whip cream — that's what gives it the light, mousse-like texture instead of a dense balm. It's soft enough to scoop with a fingertip straight from the jar.

Will it work under makeup or SPF?+

Yes — it sinks in within about sixty seconds and leaves a soft, non-greasy base. We'd still recommend a separate SPF on top, since fofo isn't formulated as sun protection.

Is it suitable for sensitive or acne-prone skin?+

The formula is fragrance-free and dye-free with only six ingredients, which makes it a low-irritation option for most sensitive skin. It's non-comedogenic, but as always, patch-test on your inner arm first if you're reactive.

How long does one jar last?+

Most people get 6–8 weeks from a 50ml jar using it once nightly. A little goes further than it looks, since the whip expands as it warms in your hands.
